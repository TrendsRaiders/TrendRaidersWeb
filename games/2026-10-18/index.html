About Fly the Simulation

A tiny cartoon fly wants a banana. Steer it up and down through seven walls, each with one opening. Three walls rebuild as they approach: the old opening fills in and the dashed NEXT GAP becomes the real one. Follow the dashed gap. Reach the banana at the end of the 20-second course.

  • Keyboard: Up/W and Down/S steer, Escape pauses. Touch or mouse: hold Fly up or Fly down.
  • Any wall contact is a bonk; Retry returns to the intro. The course is the same every time: nothing is random.
  • Pause, Resume and Restart are below the arena. Resume waits until you release the Resume key or button.
  • Win sharing is optional: Copy result copies plain text, nothing is posted for you.

This is an unofficial parody and a fictional cartoon. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Odyssey or anyone named in the news, and it makes no claim about the experiment or about what flies, brains or simulations are. All art is original and there is no sound.
