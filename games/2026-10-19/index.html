    About Bigfoot: Take Two

    You are a homemade movie monster on a suspiciously cheap forest set. The performer walks on its own; your only action is POSE when its feet reach the taped mark. A good shutter records the pose; a bad one records an embarrassing rehearsal. Five shots at most, four usable frames to win.

    • Each shot starts with a short ROLLING lead-in, then the monster walks. The bracketed strip on the floor is the usable window; the center tape is its middle.
    • Space or Enter (while focus is on the stage or POSE), the POSE button, or a tap on the stage all do the same thing. Holding a key does not repeat.
    • Pose too early, too late or not at all and the shot is a miss. Two misses end the attempt; Retry returns to this introduction. Nothing is random.
    • Pause, Restart and the optional sound toggle sit below the stage. Escape pauses while focus is in the game. Switching windows or hiding the page pauses automatically and never resumes by itself.
    • Winning shows a short optional reveal; Replay reveal never changes your result. Sharing is optional and never posts anything for you.

    This is a fictional cartoon. The cardboard forest, the crew and the costume are invented for the joke; none of it explains or recreates any real film, and the game makes no claim about whether Bigfoot exists or about any real person.
