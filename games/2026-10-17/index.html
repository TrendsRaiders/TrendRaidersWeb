About 30-Second KO

The headline lasted 30 seconds. Your rematch gets 30.

Block the hit. Punch on COUNTER. KO Jack before time runs out.

  • When Jack raises a glove (WIND-UP), hold Guard. After BLOCK, the COUNTER window opens: tap Punch.
  • Punching early drops your guard for half a second, and an unguarded hit stuns you.
  • Keyboard: Space or J punches; K or Shift holds Guard; Escape pauses.
  • Touch: tap Punch, hold Guard. Both can be used at once.
