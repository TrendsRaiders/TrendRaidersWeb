About 30-Second KO

Hold Guard for the wind-up. After the hit, tap Punch to counter. KO before 30 seconds!

  • Keyboard: Space or J punches; K or Shift holds Guard; Escape pauses.
  • Touch: tap Punch, hold Guard. Both can be used at once.
  • Punching leaves you open for half a second, and an unguarded hit stuns you.
